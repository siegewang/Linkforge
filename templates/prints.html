Desktop Protocol Handlers Setup

Enable 1-click launch for Bambu Studio & Fusion 360

How Protocol Handlers Work

When you click Open in Bambu Studio or Open in Fusion 360, your web browser invokes a custom URL scheme (bambustudio:// or fusion360://). A lightweight launcher script on your workstation automatically downloads the 3D model into your temporary cache and launches the desktop app with the model placed onto the virtual build plate.

Windows Setup (1-Click Installer)

In the LinkForge repository's desktop-handlers/windows/ folder:

  1. Right-click install_handlers.bat and select Run as administrator.
  2. The script automatically registers both bambustudio:// and fusion360:// in the Windows Registry.
  3. Return to your browser and click Bambu Studio. When prompted by your browser, check Always allow and click Open!
Linux Setup (GNOME / KDE) 
cd desktop-handlers/linux
chmod +x install_handlers.sh
./install_handlers.sh
